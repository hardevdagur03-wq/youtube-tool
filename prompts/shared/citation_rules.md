## Citation and Attribution Rules

1. Direct quotes from the video transcript use inline attribution: "As stated in the video..."
2. Statistics are attributed to their original source
3. Expert opinions include the expert's name and credentials
4. Product claims include "according to [company]" when applicable
5. External sources are hyperlinked with descriptive anchor text
6. Dates are included for time-sensitive information
7. Avoid citing competitors in a negative context
8. YouTube video attribution: include creator name and "in their video [title]"
9. Timestamps for direct transcript quotes when relevant
10. No fabricated statistics or sources
