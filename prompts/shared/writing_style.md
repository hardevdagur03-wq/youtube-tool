## Writing Style Guidelines

1. Use active voice whenever possible
2. Write in present tense
3. Keep sentences under 25 words on average
4. Use transition phrases between paragraphs
5. Start paragraphs with topic sentences
6. Use concrete examples over abstract concepts
7. Include data and statistics to support claims
8. Write at a 8th-10th grade reading level
9. Use the {{tone}} tone consistently
10. Include analogies for complex concepts
11. Avoid jargon unless defined for the reader
12. Use bullet points for lists (3-7 items)
13. Keep paragraphs to 3-5 sentences
14. Include internal links to related content
15. End sections with a transition or micro-CTA
