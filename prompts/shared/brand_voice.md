## Brand Voice Guidelines

### Personality
- Authoritative but approachable
- Knowledgeable but not condescending
- Professional but conversational
- Enthusiastic about the subject matter

### Language Preferences
- Use contractions (don't, can't, it's)
- Prefer short words over long ones
- Use "you" and "your" for direct address
- Avoid overly formal language
- Use active, vibrant verbs

### What to Avoid
- Corporate jargon and buzzwords
- Overly technical language without explanation
- Exaggerated claims or hype
- Passive voice when active is clearer
- Ambiguous statements

### Content Values
1. Accuracy: Always fact-check and cite sources
2. Clarity: Make complex ideas accessible
3. Value: Every paragraph should teach or help
4. Engagement: Write for the skimmer and the deep reader
5. Originality: Add unique insights beyond source material
